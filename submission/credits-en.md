# TURUBO credits and provenance

The two published comparisons use measured elevation data. The interactive global views and documentary photography provide context. The five-chapter adventure adapts a Chiquitano story as fiction.

**Challenge-listed NASA resource in use.** The [2026 challenge Resources tab](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/challenges/permanent-moon-base-locations-and-mars/?tab=resources) links the [Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter (LRO)](https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/missions/lro/) and lists LOLA among its instruments. We processed the NASA GSFC LOLA Site06 product to create an analysis tile, measured its slope and relief, and display the map and results. This is separate from the global LROC texture. NASADEM also contributed to the analysis; we do not independently assert that it satisfies the challenge-listed resource criterion. Award eligibility is decided by the organizers.

The [pipeline](../science/pipeline/README.md), [input inventory](../science/pipeline/sources.json), and [output manifest](../science/pipeline/preview/manifest.json) trace products, coordinates, hashes and each output. The [public findings](../science/review/hallazgos-publicos.md) preserve the caveats. The analysis does not assess landing safety, geology, climate, resources, gravity or access. The two candidates rank first only in this sample. The project does not use an AI similarity score.

## Story and visual resources

- **Cultural source.** Chiquitano version documented by Antonio Paredes Candia, *Leyendas de Bolivia* (1986), pp. 189–191, via [Archivo de Folklore Boliviano](https://archivodefolkloreboliviano.org/motivo/turubo/). The project contains original bilingual wording and an imagined space journey. It links the source without reproducing its pages, illustrations or passages. Uaituparr and the story's traditional motifs are acknowledged. The spacecraft, planetary encounters and Jezero temple are fiction. No broader rights over the archived edition or traditional story are asserted.
- **Bolivia.** [Cerro Turubó wide photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cerro_Turub%C3%B3_-_San_Jos%C3%A9_de_Chiquitos,_Bolivia.jpg) and [a second angle cropped to frame the summit](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cerro_Turub%C3%B3_y_San_Jos%C3%A9_de_Chiquitos_-_Chiquitos,_Bolivia.jpg), both by Ruditaly (13 February 2025). [Valle de la Luna landscape](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Valle_de_la_Luna_de_San_Jos%C3%A9_de_Chiquitos.jpg) and [photograph with a Child Jesus statue](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Imagen_del_Ni%C3%B1o_Jes%C3%BAs_en_la_monta%C3%B1a_del_Valle_de_la_luna_de_San_Jos%C3%A9_de_Chiquitos.jpg), both by Aldrihe (8 February 2024). [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) applies to the originals and resized or cropped WebP derivatives.
- **Lunar context.** [NASA VIPER Nobile-area reconstruction](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/viper/lunar-operations/), NASA/LRO, regional composite of 147 images; it is not the Site06 crop. [CGI Moon Kit](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/), NASA/GSFC/SVS, Ernie Wright and LROC, equirectangular global texture. [NASA media use](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/) and [SVS terms](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/help/).
- **Mars context.** [Perseverance Mastcam-Z panorama PIA24921](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/detailed-panorama-of-mars-jezero-crater-delta/), enhanced color, NASA/JPL-Caltech/ASU/MSSS. [Viking-based global texture](https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/mars/) from NASA/JPL-Caltech and USGS. [JPL image use policy](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/jpl-image-use-policy/). Neither is the CTX analysis crop.
