# TURUBO jury demo

The demonstration is a public [seven-slide English PDF](https://github.com/federicorv25/turubo/releases/download/turubo-demo-final-2026/TURUBO_Demo_3D_EN.pdf). The [editable PowerPoint](https://github.com/federicorv25/turubo/releases/download/turubo-demo-final-2026/TURUBO_Demo_3D_EN.pptx) is the source deck. The separate Final Project link is [turubo.tech](https://turubo.tech/).

1. **TURUBO: From Earth to New Worlds.** Introduce the adventure and the terrain question. Label the cover illustration as AI-generated.
2. **Two sites in Bolivia.** Explain why Valle de la Luna supplies the sample and Cerro Turubó is a separate example. Show and credit both documentary photographs.
3. **A choice in the story leads to a map.** Describe the two reactions in Asael's 3D Earth encounter. The illustrated English Moon chapter shows how players can open a measured map and return to their choice.
4. **From lunar elevations to a comparison.** Trace LRO/LOLA Site06 to the measured 4 × 4 km map. Show the common 40 m grid, coverage threshold and metrics.
5. **Nobile has 2.7× the relief.** Pair the published maps, compare slopes and relief, and explain interpolation.
6. **Jezero has similar slopes and more relief.** Pair the published maps and explain what they can tell us.
7. **The full journey.** Show the public mobile view, the project link and AI disclosure.

The demo uses actual screenshots and the exact map files served by the site. See the [credits](credits-en.md), [verification](../docs/evidence/viaje-3d.md) and [Space Apps field text](project-en.md). Publishing the demo does not submit the project for judging.
